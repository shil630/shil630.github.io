I'm Liang Shi, a Parsons MFA graduate with 10+ years of experience across leading US and China tech companies. I currently serve as an Experience Design Director and am a long-term practitioner of US equity investing and Texas Hold'em.

Here I document my Positive EV experiments: how to judge whether something is worth pursuing, how much to commit, and when to exit. Whether designing complex enterprise systems, making range decisions at the poker table, or sizing positions in public markets, the underlying framework is the same. I regularly publish US equity watchlists, real decision reviews, and ThesisSee (thesisee.com), an AI investment thesis tracker I'm building.

决策框架

EV 值不值得

先分辨信号与噪音，估算赔率与胜率，不参与负期望值的热闹。

SIZE 投入多少

根据确定性与可承受回撤决定投入尺度，让单次失误永远在系统可承受范围内。

EXIT 何时退出

在入场前写清证伪条件，当核心假设失效或逻辑兑现时按纪律离场。
